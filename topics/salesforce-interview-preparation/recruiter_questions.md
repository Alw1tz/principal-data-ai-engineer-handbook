# Recruiter Questions for Lead Agentic Data Systems Engineer

## About the Role and Team

1. **Can you tell me about the team structure and reporting relationships?**
   - What's the size of the Enterprise Data & AI Solutions group?
   - Who does this role report to directly?
   - Are there any cross-functional teams I'll be working with?

2. **What are the biggest challenges the team is currently facing?**
   - What are the top priorities for this role in the next 6-12 months?
   - How does this position contribute to broader company goals?

3. **What does success look like in this role?**
   - How is performance measured for this position?
   - What are the key deliverables expected in the first 90 days?

## About the Technology Stack

4. **What tools and technologies are used for data engineering and AI orchestration?**
   - What's the current tech stack for agentic systems?
   - Are there any specific frameworks or platforms that are emphasized (e.g., LangGraph, MCP servers)?

5. **How does the company approach AI implementation in data products?**
   - What's the organization's strategy for autonomous agents and AI-native development?
   - Are there existing AI projects or initiatives I'd be joining?

## About the Company Culture

6. **What's the company culture like around data engineering and AI?**
   - How does the company support innovation in data and AI technologies?
   - What are the opportunities for professional development in this space?

7. **How does the team handle technical challenges and problem-solving?**
   - What's the process for addressing complex technical issues?
   - How is knowledge sharing handled within the team?

## About the Interview Process

8. **What can I expect from the interview process?**
   - How many rounds are there and what's the typical timeline?
   - What type of questions or exercises should I prepare for?

9. **Are there any specific skills or experiences you're looking for that aren't explicitly mentioned in the job description?**
   - What makes a candidate stand out for this role?
   - Are there particular areas of expertise you're most interested in exploring?
