# English Vocabulary for Salesforce Interview Preparation

## Technical Terms

### Data Engineering & Architecture
- **Data Mesh** - A decentralized approach to data management and governance
- **Data-as-a-Product (DaaP)** - Treating data as a product with defined ownership and quality standards
- **Event-Driven Architecture** - An architectural pattern where systems react to events rather than being called directly
- **Semantic Layer** - A layer that provides business context and meaning to raw data
- **Data Lineage** - Tracking the flow of data from source to destination
- **Data Governance** - The management of data availability, usability, integrity, and security
- **Data Quality** - The measure of data's accuracy, completeness, and consistency
- **Data Catalog** - A centralized repository that organizes and describes data assets
- **ETL/ELT** - Extract, Transform, Load or Extract, Load, Transform processes

### AI & Agent Systems
- **Autonomous Agents** - Software entities that can operate independently to achieve goals
- **Chain-of-Thought Prompting** - A technique where models are prompted to generate intermediate reasoning steps
- **Self-Correction Loops** - Mechanisms that allow agents to detect and correct their own errors
- **Iterative Reasoning** - The process of refining conclusions through multiple iterations
- **Model Context Protocol (MCP)** - A protocol for providing secure, deep-link access to tools and data for AI agents
- **Agent Orchestration** - The coordination and management of multiple autonomous agents
- **Prompt Engineering** - The practice of designing prompts to get desired responses from AI models
- **Agentic Frameworks** - Software frameworks designed for building and managing AI agents

### Cloud & Infrastructure
- **Serverless Computing** - A cloud computing model where the cloud provider dynamically manages the allocation of machine resources
- **Microservices Architecture** - An architectural approach where applications are built as loosely coupled services
- **Containerization** - The process of packaging applications and their dependencies into containers
- **Orchestration** - The automated arrangement, coordination, and management of complex computer systems, middleware, and services

## Communication & Soft Skills

### Leadership & Management
- **Strategic Autonomy** - The ability to operate independently with minimal supervision
- **Domain Data Officer** - A role focused on managing end-to-end data strategy for a business unit
- **Technical Intuition** - The ability to quickly understand and solve technical problems
- **Operational Leverage** - Using tools or techniques to increase productivity or effectiveness

### Problem-Solving & Innovation
- **Ambiguous Requirements** - Unclear or incomplete specifications that require interpretation
- **Technical Debt** - The implied cost of additional rework caused by choosing an easy solution now instead of using a better approach that would take longer
- **Iterative Development** - A process where progress is made through repeated cycles
- **Cross-Functional Collaboration** - Working with people from different departments or specialties

### Business & Product
- **Executive Decision-Making** - The process of making important business decisions at the highest levels
- **Business Unit** - A distinct part of an organization that has its own goals and resources
- **Cognitive Automation** - Using AI to automate tasks that require human-like thinking
- **Data Product** - A data asset that delivers value to end users in a consumable format

## Behavioral Interview Vocabulary

### Demonstrating Experience
- **End-to-End Ownership** - Taking complete responsibility for a project or product from start to finish
- **Production-Grade** - Quality level suitable for live, production use
- **Hardened Systems** - Robust systems that have been tested and validated for reliability
- **Domain Knowledge** - Deep understanding of a specific field or area

### Problem-Solving Approaches
- **Challenging Design** - Critically evaluating existing designs to identify improvements
- **Implementation Reality** - The practical constraints and realities when putting plans into action
- **Strategic Vision** - Having a clear long-term perspective on goals and direction
- **Continuous Uptime** - Systems that operate reliably with minimal downtime

### Team Collaboration
- **Hybrid Human-Agent Intelligence** - Combining human expertise with AI capabilities
- **Central Anchor** - A key point of coordination or reference for a team or system
- **Knowledge Sharing** - The practice of distributing information and insights within a team
- **Cross-Functional Partnerships** - Collaborative relationships between different departments

## Interview-Specific Phrases

### Describing Your Approach
- "I take a depth-first approach to engineering"
- "I focus on building systems that can operate autonomously"
- "My primary leverage comes from disciplined building rather than broad orchestration"
- "I specialize in transforming ambiguous business requirements into production-ready solutions"

### Discussing Challenges
- "The biggest challenge is balancing autonomy with governance"
- "We need to ensure our agents are well governed and properly validated"
- "One of the key obstacles is integrating new AI capabilities with existing infrastructure"
- "I approach technical debt by implementing systematic refactoring strategies"

### Demonstrating Value
- "I've documented a history of using generative AI to accelerate output"
- "I can function as a Domain Data Officer with minimal supervision"
- "My analytical judgment helps identify subtle logic errors before they reach production"
- "I build self-healing digital immune systems for the company's data"

These vocabulary terms will help you communicate more effectively during your Salesforce interview, particularly when discussing technical concepts, problem-solving approaches, and your experience in the field of data engineering and AI systems.
