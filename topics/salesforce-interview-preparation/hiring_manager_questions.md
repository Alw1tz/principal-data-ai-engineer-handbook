# Hiring Manager Questions for Lead Agentic Data Systems Engineer

## Role and Expectations

1. **What are the primary goals and deliverables for this role in the first 6 months?**
   - How will success be measured for this position?
   - What specific outcomes would you like to see from someone in this role?

2. **How does this role contribute to the broader data strategy of the organization?**
   - What are the strategic priorities that this position directly supports?
   - How do you see this role evolving over time?

3. **What are the biggest technical challenges currently facing the team?**
   - What problems are we trying to solve with autonomous agents and AI orchestration?
   - What are the key obstacles in implementing agentic systems at scale?

## Technical Approach and Culture

4. **What's your approach to building and maintaining autonomous agent ecosystems?**
   - How do you balance autonomy with governance in agent development?
   - What principles guide your decisions about when to use agents vs. traditional approaches?

5. **How does the team handle technical debt and legacy systems?**
   - What strategies are used for integrating new AI capabilities with existing infrastructure?
   - How do you approach modernizing legacy data systems with agentic solutions?

6. **What's the organization's philosophy around AI-native development?**
   - How do you see the role of generative AI in data engineering workflows?
   - What tools or frameworks are you most excited about implementing?

## Team and Collaboration

7. **How does this role collaborate with other teams (e.g., product, business, security)?**
   - What's the typical workflow for cross-functional projects?
   - How do you ensure alignment between technical capabilities and business needs?

8. **What does a typical day look like for someone in this role?**
   - What proportion of time is spent on hands-on engineering vs. strategic thinking?
   - How do you balance immediate project demands with long-term architectural vision?

## Technical Depth and Expertise

9. **What specific technical skills are most critical for success in this role?**
   - Which technologies or frameworks would you want someone to be deeply proficient in?
   - What knowledge areas should I focus on to prepare for this position?

10. **How does the team approach problem-solving with complex, ambiguous requirements?**
    - What's your process for translating high-level business needs into technical solutions?
    - How do you handle situations where requirements are unclear or change frequently?

## Growth and Development

11. **What opportunities exist for professional growth in this role?**
    - Are there plans for expanding the scope of this position?
    - What are the career advancement paths available within the data team?

12. **What would be your ideal candidate's experience in AI orchestration?**
    - What specific examples of agentic systems or autonomous workflows have you seen work well?
    - How do you evaluate the effectiveness of agent-based solutions?
