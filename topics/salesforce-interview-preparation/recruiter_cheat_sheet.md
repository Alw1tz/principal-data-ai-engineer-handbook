# Recruiter Cheat Sheet for Lead Agentic Data Systems Engineer

## Key Information to Gather

### Role Details
- **Position Title**: Lead Agentic Data Systems Engineer
- **Location**: Mexico City | Full-Time | Hybrid
- **Department**: Enterprise Data & AI Solutions group
- **Team Size**: Strategic hub for cognitive automation
- **Reporting Structure**: Direct reports to Principal Engineers

### Core Responsibilities
1. **Autonomous Scaling**: Architect and maintain a private ecosystem of 10+ autonomous agents
2. **Agentic Orchestration**: Design multi-step reasoning architectures and verification protocols
3. **Complex Problem Resolution**: Transform high-level business requirements into production-ready data products
4. **Governance & Oversight**: Ensure deployed tools are well governed with governance as code
5. **Contextual Integration**: Develop Model Context Protocol (MCP) servers for secure access

### Technical Requirements
- **Engineering Foundation**: Production-grade proficiency in Python, dbt, Airflow, advanced SQL, Spark, Snowflake
- **AI Orchestration**: Fluency in AI-native development environments and expert in Prompt Engineering
- **Cognitive Architecture**: Expert-level knowledge of chain-of-thought prompting, self-correction loops, iterative reasoning paths
- **Salesforce Knowledge**: Salesforce Core and Data 360 understanding
- **Systems Design**: Advanced understanding of Data Mesh, Data-as-a-Product (DaaP), Event-Driven Architectures

### Key Technologies Mentioned
- **Agentic Frameworks**: LangGraph
- **Protocol**: Model Control Protocol (MCP)
- **Data Platforms**: Snowflake, Salesforce, AWS
- **Data Engineering Tools**: Spark, Airflow, dbt, Python
- **Infrastructure**: Docker, Kubernetes, serverless compute environments

## What to Ask Recruiters

### About the Role
1. "Can you elaborate on what 'autonomous scaling' means in practice for this role?"
2. "What specific challenges is the team currently facing with autonomous agents?"
3. "How does this position contribute to the broader company strategy?"

### About the Technology Stack
4. "What's the current tech stack for agentic systems in this group?"
5. "Are there any specific frameworks or platforms that are emphasized (e.g., LangGraph, MCP servers)?"
6. "How do you approach AI implementation in data products?"

### About Team and Culture
7. "What's the team structure and reporting relationships like?"
8. "How does the company support innovation in data and AI technologies?"
9. "What's the process for addressing complex technical issues?"

### About Expectations
10. "What does success look like in this role over the first 6-12 months?"
11. "How is performance measured for this position?"
12. "What are the key deliverables expected in the first 90 days?"

## Red Flags to Watch For

### Technical Depth
- If they don't understand or value technical expertise
- No discussion of specific tools (Spark, Snowflake, Airflow)
- Lack of awareness about agentic systems and autonomous agents

### Company Culture
- Inconsistent communication or delayed responses
- Vague descriptions of team structure or role expectations
- No clear explanation of how the role contributes to company goals

### Compensation & Growth
- Unrealistic salary expectations for a senior role
- No discussion of career development opportunities
- Limited mentorship or learning programs

## Key Points to Communicate

### Your Value Proposition
1. **End-to-end Ownership**: "I take complete responsibility for data products from conception to production"
2. **Technical Depth**: "My primary leverage comes from deep, disciplined building rather than broad orchestration"
3. **Autonomous Systems**: "I specialize in transforming ambiguous business requirements into production-ready solutions"
4. **AI Integration**: "I've documented a history of using generative AI to accelerate personal and departmental output"

### Your Experience Areas
1. **Data Engineering**: Spark, Snowflake, Airflow, dbt, Python
2. **AI Orchestration**: LangGraph, Prompt Engineering, agentic frameworks
3. **Systems Design**: Data Mesh, Event-Driven Architectures, Data-as-a-Product
4. **Governance**: Data governance as code, secure agent development

## Interview Preparation Tips

### Research the Company
1. Understand their approach to cognitive automation and data engineering
2. Learn about their current projects or initiatives in AI/ML
3. Research their technology stack and infrastructure
4. Understand their business goals and how data supports them

### Prepare Examples
1. **Autonomous Systems**: Have stories about building self-sufficient agents
2. **Data Pipelines**: Examples of scalable, reliable data workflows
3. **AI Integration**: Projects where you integrated AI capabilities with existing systems
4. **Governance**: Experience with data quality and security in autonomous environments

### Practice Communication
1. Articulate complex technical concepts clearly
2. Connect technical work to business outcomes
3. Demonstrate strategic thinking about data engineering
4. Show understanding of both technical depth and business context

## Follow-Up Questions

1. "What are the biggest challenges the team is currently facing?"
2. "How does this role collaborate with other teams (product, business, security)?"
3. "What's your approach to building and maintaining autonomous agent ecosystems?"
4. "How do you handle technical debt and legacy systems in AI environments?"
5. "What opportunities exist for professional growth in this role?"
6. "How does the team approach problem-solving with complex, ambiguous requirements?"

This cheat sheet will help you stay focused during recruiter conversations and ensure you're gathering all the important information while also presenting your value as a Lead Agentic Data Systems Engineer.
